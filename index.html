WellRadio Listing Manager

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

BAIX TRADE CO., LIMITED operates WellRadio Listing Manager, an internal tool for managing our own eBay seller account. This policy describes how we handle information accessed through eBay APIs and information provided when contacting us.

Information we access

Depending on the permissions granted and the functions used, the application may access seller account and profile information, listings, inventory, offers, account settings, and order and fulfillment information. Order information may include buyer contact and delivery information where eBay permits access and it is necessary to process an order. We also handle OAuth authorization tokens and information you provide by email. The application does not ask for or store your eBay account password.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate access to our seller account, maintain listings and inventory, retrieve and fulfill orders, provide order-related customer service, and investigate operational problems. We use personal information only as necessary for these purposes and as permitted by eBay. We do not sell personal information or use eBay personal information for unrelated advertising or to train AI models.

Access, storage and security

Access to seller data is restricted to authorized personnel and services needed to operate the tool. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in an encrypted password database and encrypted local credential cache. Operational data may be held in internal databases or business tools used for store management. We use access controls and limit the information handled to what the relevant operation requires.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be shared with eBay and, where needed, service providers supporting our operations, such as hosting, business tools, and delivery providers. Information is shared only for the relevant service or where required by law. This public policy page does not display seller credentials, tokens, or order data.

Retention and deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for the stated purposes and applicable recordkeeping requirements. We delete information when it is no longer needed, when required by eBay, or in response to a valid request, subject to applicable legal requirements. Revoking access stops future authorized API access; it does not automatically erase records already held.

Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. We may need to verify your identity before responding. An authorizing eBay account holder can revoke application access in their eBay account's third-party app access settings. Please do not send account passwords or authorization tokens by email.

This website

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, tracking scripts, cookies, or forms to this page. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests, contact BAIX TRADE CO., LIMITED at baixiang816@163.com. We may update this policy as the application changes. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.
